# Backend Observability

## Global requirements

- Structured logs with correlation/request IDs.
- Metrics for traffic, latency, errors, dependencies, queues, and critical business events.
- Tracing across API, worker, database, and external calls where useful.
- Error reporting and alerts with a named dashboard/runbook owner.
- No secrets or unnecessary personal data in logs, traces, metrics, or alerts.
